## Why this lesson matters

Most companies no longer give every human a long-lived IAM user with access keys. Identity Center (successor to AWS SSO) is how workforce users sign in to multiple accounts with temporary credentials.

## Learning goals (preview)

- Explain IAM Identity Center vs IAM users
- Describe permission sets and account assignments
- Know when orgs choose SSO for workforce access
